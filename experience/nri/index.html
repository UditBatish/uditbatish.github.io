Nomura (NRI)

Investment Strategy Consulting · Associate Consultant, then Senior Associate Consultant

Nomura (NRI) is one of Japan's top management consulting firms.

I first joined Nomura (NRI) as a summer intern in 2023, got a PPO at the end of it, and joined full-time after graduating. I started as an Associate Consultant and was promoted to Senior Associate Consultant in April 2025.

NRI was a great place to learn the ropes and build a strong foundation after college. I got to work with some incredibly talented people and had a fantastic experience. Eventually, in 2025, I decided to move on to get closer to the technology ecosystem.

My Work

My work at NRI was largely across two areas:

  1. Strategy Projects

    Worked with global consumer and industrial companies on market entry, go-to-market strategy, and business feasibility. This involved understanding customers, sizing markets, benchmarking competitors, figuring out distribution strategies, and building business cases, particularly for companies looking to enter India.

  2. Transaction Projects

    Worked on commercial due diligence for investors evaluating Indian businesses, stress-testing business plans through industry research, expert interviews, demand forecasting, and financial analysis. Also worked on sell-side advisory mandates, helping companies put together business plans and engage with potential strategic investors.

One of my favourite highlights, though, was gaslighting the team into believing running is fun and getting everyone to sign up for a race/marathon!
