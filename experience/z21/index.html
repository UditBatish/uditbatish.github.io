Z21 Ventures

Investment Analyst

z21 Ventures is a Silicon Valley-based, operator-led VC firm investing in pre-seed and seed-stage Enterprise AI and deep-tech startups across the U.S.

As part of the investment team, I've had the opportunity to work closely with our three co-founders and partners (Sudarshan, Jyotika and Raj) across pretty much every aspect of running an early-stage VC fund. It's been an incredible experience getting to work alongside some of the brightest founders building in what I believe is one of the most consequential technological shifts of our generation.

Investments

  1. Investment Execution

    Supported end-to-end investment execution across four companies spanning AI-native insurance brokerage, construction robotics, text-to-SQL and supersonic UAVs. I particularly enjoy evaluating unconventional ideas and working with founders building at the intersection of AI and large, traditional industries.

  2. Founder Sourcing & Network

    Led 100+ founder first calls and built an active sourcing pipeline through relationships with founders, operators and VCs. I enjoy meeting ambitious founders early.

Focus Thematics

A big part of my role involves going deep into emerging technologies and developing investment theses. Two areas I've been particularly excited about:

  1. Enterprise AI

    I'm excited about AI moving beyond copilots to owning entire workflows and replacing significant chunks of labour. I'm particularly interested in vertical AI companies building deep, industry-specific solutions with strong workflow integration and defensibility.

  2. Physical AI & Robotics

    I'm fascinated by AI moving from the digital world into the physical one. The convergence of foundation models, cheaper hardware and advances in robotics could unlock entirely new categories of automation.

If you're building something exciting in either of these areas or simply want to exchange notes, reach out at udit.batish@z21.ventures.

Beyond Investments

  1. Fund Operations & AI Tooling

    I've been building AI-native internal tools to automate sourcing, diligence, CRM management and our investment knowledge base. It's been fun experimenting with how AI can help a small investment team operate with significantly more leverage.

  2. Portfolio Support

    Worked closely with our portfolio companies on post-investment value creation, including hiring, GTM partnerships and fundraising.
